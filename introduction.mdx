Lightdash is an open-source business intelligence platform built for dbt teams. Instead of defining metrics in a separate BI tool, you declare dimensions and metrics directly in your dbt YAML files — and Lightdash automatically surfaces them as a self-service analytics layer for everyone in your organization.

## Why Lightdash?

Most BI tools require you to redefine your business logic in a separate layer — meaning your data team maintains the same metrics in dbt *and* in the BI tool, which inevitably drift apart. Lightdash eliminates this duplication.

- **Single source of truth** — metrics and dimensions live in your dbt YAML, versioned alongside your data models
- **Self-service analytics** — business users explore data through pre-defined dimensions and metrics without writing SQL
- **Developer-native workflow** — preview BI environments, content-as-code, and CI/CD validation fit naturally into existing dbt workflows
- **Open source** — fully open source under a community license, with an optional cloud offering

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your dbt project">
    Point Lightdash at your dbt project (cloud or CLI) and your data warehouse. Lightdash reads your `manifest.json` to discover all models, dimensions, and metrics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define metrics in dbt YAML">
    Add `meta` blocks to your dbt model YAML files to declare dimensions, metrics, and joins. Lightdash automatically picks up these definitions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore and visualize">
    Use the Lightdash Explorer to build charts, apply filters, and create dashboards — all backed by your dbt-defined business logic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share and schedule">
    Organize charts into spaces, set permissions, and schedule automated deliveries to Slack or email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported data warehouses

Lightdash connects to all major data warehouses out of the box:

- **BigQuery** — Google Cloud BigQuery
- **Snowflake** — Snowflake Data Cloud
- **PostgreSQL** — including Amazon RDS and Aurora
- **Amazon Redshift**
- **Databricks** — Databricks SQL Warehouses
- **Amazon Athena**
- **ClickHouse**
- **Trino**
- **DuckDB** — for local development and testing
